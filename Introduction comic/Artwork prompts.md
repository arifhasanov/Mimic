## Shared art direction

Use case: illustration-story. Create one finished landscape 16:9 comic book page for MIMIC, a science fiction social deduction game. Mature atmospheric graphic novel, hand-inked contours, painterly detail, restrained halftone texture, cinematic readable compositions, weathered cream industrial equipment, charcoal shadows, teal glass and amber work lights. No text, lettering, speech bubbles, labels, logos or watermarks. Panels separated by thin dark gutters. Keep the action fully visible with breathing space. Ten adult miners in practical matching worn ochre work suits, dark boots and clear-visored helmets; distinguish the two targeted miners by an off-white shoulder stripe and a rust-red shoulder stripe. Aliens are exactly two small shadowy ink-black fluid organisms with subtle tendrils, capable of perfectly copying humans including outfits. Non-graphic suspense, no blood or exposed anatomy. Ship reference is an APPROXIMATE DESIGN REFERENCE ONLY: top view with roof removed to expose interior, NOT an exterior open roof. Exterior ship must have closed weathered cream hull, elongated squat industrial silhouette, broad squared rear left, rounded teal-glazed cockpit nose right, asymmetrical rectangular medbay side extension that stops behind cockpit; exactly FOUR rear engines in a row ACROSS THE WIDTH (NOT vertically stacked): three equal large cylindrical nozzles and a fourth smaller end nozzle by medbay side. No wings, no added engines. Keep this design consistent. Medbay reference: cream oval upright scanning ring at one end of a horizontal teal-padded examination bed, exposed pipes, overhead surgical lamps.

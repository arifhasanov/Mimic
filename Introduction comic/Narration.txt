PAGE 1 — THE HAUL
We came for what the planet held beneath its surface. Minerals. Metals. Enough to fill the hold. Ten miners, one ship, and one last haul before home.

PAGE 2 — BEHIND THE STONES
Beyond the landing lights, two things watched us work. When two of our crew strayed behind the stones, the things hid. Then they struck.

PAGE 3 — BORROWED FACES
They entered our crewmates and learned their shapes. Their faces. Their size. Even their clothes. What walked back to the ship looked exactly like the people we knew.

PAGE 4 — TEN ABOARD
We counted ten aboard. Closed the hatch. Left the planet behind. No one saw the two bodies lying in the dust, beyond the reach of our landing lights.

PAGE 5 — SOMETHING WRONG
Out among the stars, things began to fail. Something was wrong aboard our ship. The medbay scanner could show what a face could hide. We would repair it, then check everyone. One by one.

PAGE 6 — PROOF OF HUMANITY
We rebuilt the X-ray device. Every connection restored. Every test passed. At last, we had a way to tell who was human. For a moment, we thought we were safe.

PAGE 7 — SABOTAGE
Then the medbay emptied. One crewmate slipped back inside. A familiar hand became something else. Wires snapped. The scanner went dark. Something aboard did not want to be found.
